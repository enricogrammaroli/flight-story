A change story · LIS 2188 · Module 9.2

Same commute,
different doors

In the past 18 months, for work and research projects, I have taken an unprecedented number of flights. For this part of the 2188 class activities, I worked with data from these flights, and from the start I wanted to play with it and present it as a visualization on a map.

In this way, I realized that this looks like a sort of commute over the Atlantic that brings to the same cities, passing through a lot of others.

Four seasons, four doors

How the route changed

The endpoints stay fixed while the connection keeps moving. Each season settles on one carrier family and one hub, then switches. Select a season to isolate it on the globe.

The essential questions

Who
Classmates and instructor; anyone who lives between two places.
Why
To show how a fixed commute is shaped by airline networks, not by the traveller.
What
A personal flight log: 35 legs, airports, carriers and coordinates.
When
9 Sep 2025 to 31 Oct 2026.
Where
Pittsburgh and Rome, with 13 other airports in between.
How
Great-circle arcs on a globe, a timeline and four season cards.

What the data cannot tell

  • Why each route was chosen: price, schedule, loyalty programme or a specific trip purpose.
  • How Cluj-Napoca (5 Oct) connects to Athens (28 Oct). That leg is missing from the log.
  • Departure times, delays, ground travel and layover length.
  • Distances are straight great-circle lines, not the paths the aircraft flew.
  • Emissions: no CO₂ figure is shown, because cabin class and aircraft type are not in the log.

Responsible storytelling

  • Positionality. The author is also the subject. The log reflects one person's mobility and the privilege that comes with it.
  • Boundaries. The "community" here is a single traveller; nothing should be generalised from it.
  • Privacy. Exact dates of presence are personal data. They are shared here by choice, at day level only.
  • Status. The last three flights were still bookings on 24 October 2026.
